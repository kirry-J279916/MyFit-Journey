What is MyFit Journey?

Your one source for eating well, moving more and finding a coach near you.

About Us

MyFit Journey helps you start small and keep going. You can try easy recipes, work out how many calories you need, and apply to be matched with a local coach.

Why It Helps

In 2022, 66% of Australian adults aged 18 and over were living with overweight or obesity (Source: AIHW). Small changes can help.

  • More energy through the day
  • Better sleep
  • A better mood and less stress
  • A healthier heart
