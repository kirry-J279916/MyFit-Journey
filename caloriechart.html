Calorie Chart

This table shows roughly how many calories an adult needs each day to stay at the same weight.

Estimated daily calories to maintain weight with office worker lifestyle (kcal per day)
Body weight Women Men
50-59 kg 1,670-2,060 1,990-2,420
60-69 kg 1,810-2,220 2,130-2,580
70-79 kg 1,950-2,380 2,270-2,740
80-89 kg 2,090-2,540 2,410-2,900
90-99 kg 2,230-2,700 2,550-3,060
100+ kg 2,370 or more 2,690 or more

These are rough estimates for an adult with an office job, to stay at the same weight. Your own needs may be different. This is a general guide, not personal dietary advice. Background: Eat For Health: Dietary energy
